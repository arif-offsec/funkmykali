# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as contributors and maintainers of **funkmykali** pledge to make participation in our project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We're building a tool for the security community — students, career-changers, and working professionals alike. Everyone starts somewhere, and questions from beginners are as welcome here as pull requests from veterans.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the project
- Showing empathy towards other contributors and users
- Helping newcomers who are just starting their pentesting/cert journey

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments — public or private
- Trolling, and deliberately disruptive or bad-faith contributions
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Gatekeeping — mocking someone for not knowing a tool or technique
- Any conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces — GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions — and also applies when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces (e.g. posting about funkmykali on Reddit, Discord, or elsewhere).

## Responsible & Ethical Use

funkmykali installs offensive security tooling. This project exists for **authorized** penetration testing, certification labs (HTB, OffSec, TCM Security, INE, TryHackMe), and CTF practice only.

- Do not use these tools against systems you don't own or don't have explicit written authorization to test.
- Do not open issues or discussions requesting help attacking real-world targets without authorization.
- Contributions that add functionality primarily useful for unauthorized access, fraud, or harming others will be rejected.

Misuse of these tools is the sole responsibility of the user, not the maintainers or contributors of this project.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by opening a GitHub issue tagged `conduct`, or by contacting the maintainer directly via GitHub ([@arif-offsec](https://github.com/arif-offsec)).

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

Project maintainers have the right to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban any contributor for behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.
